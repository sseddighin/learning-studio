01 / Our approach

Less watching the future.
More shaping it together.

Learning Studio is an independent, fictional creative learning collective. We believe the best ideas happen when people from different disciplines roll up their sleeves and try something new.

Our hands-on programs make emerging technology approachable, with space to ask better questions, make a few mistakes, and build something meaningful. People first. Possibility always.

An independent concept. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of California, Irvine (UCI).
