01 / Our approach

Less watching the future.
More shaping it together.

Future Learning Studio is a fictional university initiative for teaching innovation, learning research, and community collaboration. We believe the best ideas happen when people from different disciplines roll up their sleeves and try something new.

Our hands-on programs make emerging technology approachable, with space to ask better questions, make a few mistakes, and build something meaningful. Faculty test new teaching approaches, researchers investigate how people learn, and community partners help connect inquiry to everyday challenges.

An independent concept. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of California, Irvine (UCI).
